# Blinkit-Operations-Dashboard
Interactive dashboard built using Power BI to analyze BlinkIT grocery sales performance. It provides insights into total sales, average sales, item ratings, item visibility, outlet establishment trends, outlet size, location, and product categories. Designed to support data-driven business decisions through clear visualizations and KPI tracking.

**Insights**
Demand is strongest in Tier 3 markets, suggesting significant growth opportunities beyond major urban centers.
Medium-sized outlets appear to provide the best balance between operational efficiency and revenue generation.
The dominance of Supermarket Type 1 indicates that this format is the most effective business model and can be prioritized for future expansion.
High-performing categories such as Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods should receive greater inventory allocation and promotional focus.
Underperforming categories may require targeted marketing, assortment optimization, or pricing strategies to improve sales contribution.
Consistent ratings across outlet types indicate a relatively uniform customer experience and service quality.
The higher contribution of regular-fat products suggests that consumer purchasing behavior is driven more by taste and preference than health-oriented alternatives.

**Recommendations**
Expand operations in Tier 3 regions where sales performance is strongest.
Increase stock availability for top-selling categories to reduce lost sales opportunities.
Replicate the operational model of Supermarket Type 1 in future store rollouts.
Investigate the factors behind the 2018 sales peak and apply successful strategies to current operations.
Develop targeted promotions for low-performing categories to diversify revenue streams.
Leverage customer ratings data to identify specific areas for improving satisfaction beyond the current average of 3.9.

**Summary**
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of BlinkIT's grocery sales performance, highlighting $1.20M in total sales across 8,523 products with an average customer rating of 3.9. The analysis shows that Tier 3 locations and medium-sized outlets generate the highest revenue, while Supermarket Type 1 is the most successful outlet format. Product categories such as Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods contribute the most to sales, and regular-fat products significantly outperform low-fat alternatives. Overall, the dashboard offers valuable insights into sales trends, customer preferences, and outlet performance to support data-driven business decisions.
